05How I work

The principles I bring to every team, regardless of product or stage.

01

Start with the problem

Whether defining a feature or designing a data pipeline, the first job is getting the core question right. I dig into the “why” until the problem space is sharp and unambiguous.

02

Use data and judgment

Data science taught me how to measure and validate. Qualitative research and marketing taught me that behavior rarely fits into spreadsheets. I balance both to build the right thing.

03

Ship small, learn fast

I favor testing ideas early. I’d rather test a focused idea, see what we learn, and adjust than spend months building something we haven’t validated.

04

Build common ground

Every function speaks its own language. I help teams work through ambiguity, surface the important questions, and build toward a shared direction.
